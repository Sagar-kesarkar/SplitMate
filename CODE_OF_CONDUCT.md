# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, especially around financial and contact data.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes professionally.
- Keep public discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. The owner may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
